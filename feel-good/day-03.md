# Day 3 of 21 Ways to Feel Good About Yourself

Yesterday you focused on doing things that will lead to success - and I hope you found it... successful! And for Day 3, we'll be focusing on a positive way to feel and act...

## Love.

Practice loving kindness for everyone, including (especially?!) neutral and difficult people. Feelings of love neutralize feelings of shame. Love protects and fuels us. Feeling loving toward others keeps us out of contentiousness and conflict and gives us a feeling of worth. When we feel fed and carried by love, we are less concerned about how others respond—accepting that it is out of our hands and we will choose to feel loving regardless.
