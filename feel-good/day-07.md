# Day 7 of 21 Ways to Feel Good About Yourself

If you're reading this, I want to congratulate and thank you for sticking with this series for a whole week! I really hope you've found the daily prompts helpful and that you're starting to feel better about yourself. And this prompt may be especially fitting for where we are in this series...

## Accept where you are in the four natural, unavoidable stages of learning and getting better at anything:

1. Unconscious Incompetence (you don't know that you don't know - you're blissfully ignorant!)

2. Conscious Incompetence (you know that you don't know - but you also see what you need to progress.)

3. Conscious Competence (you know that you know, and you're more familiar with the skill, and honing it in.)

4. Unconscious Competence (you don't know that you know - you've arrived! Doing this thing has become automatic and easy!)

The second stage – conscious incompetence – is the hardest one, and it’s where people are prone to quit. If you're there, just keep going toward growing competence, which will support your sense of worth. Remind yourself of the rewards you'll reap once you get to stages 3 and 4.
