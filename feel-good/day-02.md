# Day 2 of 21 Ways to Feel Good About Yourself

I hope the first day of the 21 day challenge for Feeling Good About Yourself went well, and you reaped the benefits of doing the right thing. And now onto Day 2...

## Tend to the causes of accomplishment.

Do the things that will legitimately earn you success. All you can do is feed the fruit tree; you can’t make it give you an apple. Take initiative, be “ardent, diligent, resolute and mindful,” and be at peace with whatever happens. As Meher Baba said: “Don’t worry. Be happy. Make efforts.” TIP: As you go through your day, think about your actions. Take a moment to think - will spending time on this produce some sort of positive outcome?
